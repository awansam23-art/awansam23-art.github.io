Fluff Up Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Fluff Up mobile game ("the app") uses, made by Sam, an independent developer ("we", "us"). It applies to the app on Android and iPhone.

Information we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Your game progress (best score, chosen world, sound setting and number of games played) is saved only on your own device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK inside the app automatically collects and shares this information with Google:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID or Apple's IDFA, if you allow it) and app set ID
  • Your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (city or region)
  • How you interact with ads and the app, such as app launches, taps and video views
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time and performance

This information is encrypted while it is sent. Google uses it as described in Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and in "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

Your choices

  • Consent (Europe, UK and other regions where required): the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy choices button in the game's Settings.
  • iPhone: the app asks for permission before tracking you through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Settings › Google › Ads on some phones).

If you choose not to allow personalized ads, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
